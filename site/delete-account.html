Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your AssetDiary account and its data at any time, without installing or opening the app.

Before you publish this Google Play requires this page to be reachable without installing the app, and requires that a request made here is actually honoured. Complete the bracketed fields, and make sure someone monitors the mailbox below.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@assetdiary.in from any address.
  2. Include your Family ID and your username so we can identify the account.
  3. We will verify the request with your family administrator before deleting anything, so that one member cannot erase another member's records.

Deleting a whole family

If you are the family administrator and want the entire family group removed — every member, every record and every document — say so in your email. This cannot be undone, and it affects every member of that family.
