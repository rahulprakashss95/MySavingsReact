AssetDiary (“the app”) is operated by [LEGAL NAME / SOLE PROPRIETOR], contactable at support@assetdiary.in. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What the app stores

Everything below is entered by you. The app does not buy, import or infer data about you from any other source.

What we do not collect

  • No email address. The app signs you in by username within your family. It never asks for an email, and none is stored.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no analytics SDK, advertising SDK or third-party tracker in the app.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, microphone or call data.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and photo library — only when you attach a scan or photo to a record, or set a profile photo.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint / Face ID) — only to unlock the app. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never visible to us; the operating system simply tells the app whether the check passed.

Who can see your data

Your records are visible to members of your family group, according to the module access your family administrator grants each member. They are not visible to other families using the app.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or disclose it to third parties, except where we are legally required to do so.

Service providers

The app stores data using Supabase, which provides our database, file storage and authentication. Supabase processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. [ADD ANY OTHER PROVIDERS, e.g. hosting, crash reporting]

How long we keep it

Your records are kept until you delete them, or until your account is deleted. See Delete your account for how to request removal and what happens afterwards.

Your rights

  • Access — you can see all of your records inside the app at any time.
  • Correction — every record can be edited in the app.
  • Deletion — request removal of your account and its data, in-app or via the web form.
  • Withdrawal — you may stop using the app and request deletion at any time.

Children

A family administrator may create accounts for members of their own household, including minors, and controls what each member can see. The app is not directed at children independently of a family administrator. [REVIEW THIS AGAINST YOUR PLAY STORE TARGET-AUDIENCE DECLARATION]
